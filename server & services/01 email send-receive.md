# Email Send and Receive

## Overview
This document demonstrates a successful email exchange using a custom domain (`rahj.ca`) through Microsoft Outlook Web Access. The exchange is between Aaron Hayes(Student) and Alice Green(Instructor), both using email addresses under the custom domain.

---

## Step 1: Aaron Sends Email to Alice
**From:** aaron.hayes@student.rahj.ca 
**To:** alice.green@rahj.ca  
**Subject:** Absent This Week Due to Illness  

Aaron notifies Ms. Green about his COVID-related absence.

![Send-Aaron to Green](images/01%20-%20Send-Aaron%20to%20Green.png)

---

## Step 2: Alice Receives Email from Aaron
**To:** alice.green@rahj.ca  
**From:** aaron.hayes@student.rahj.ca  

Ms. Green successfully receives the email in her inbox. This confirms that email delivery is working within the domain.

![Receive-Alice fr Aaron](images/02%20-%20Receive-Alice%20fr%20Aaron.png)

---

## Step 3: Alice Replies to Aaron
**From:** alice.green@rahj.ca  
**To:** aaron.hayes@student.rahj.ca  

Ms. Green sends a reply.

![Send-Green to Aaron](images/03%20-%20Send-Green%20to%20Aaron.png)

---

## Step 4: Aaron Receives Reply from Alice
**To:** aaron.hayes@student.rahj.ca  
**From:** alice.green@rahj.ca  

Aaron successfully receives the response in his inbox, confirming that two-way communication is functioning properly within the custom domain.

![Receive-Aaron fr Alice](images/04%20-%20Receive-Aaron%20fr%20Alice.png)

---

## Summary
All email exchanges were successfully completed between two users on the `rahj.ca` custom domain, demonstrating a fully functional email send and receive setup via Microsoft 365.

**Accounts Used:**  
- `aaron.hayes@student.rahj.ca`  
- `alice.green@rahj.ca`

**Service:** Microsoft Outlook Web Access (Office 365)

**Result:** Custom domain email functionality confirmed
